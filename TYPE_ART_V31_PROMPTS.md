Status: ART SOT / generation pending

## A. Shared visual grammar
Axis semantics:
- F (Future): protective / storage / forward-looking motif; calm upward/back light.
- V (Value-now): experience / reward / present-action motif; forward motion and lively foreground.
- P (Plan): ordered geometry, map/ledger/grid, composed posture.
- I (Intuition): organic shapes, dynamic posture, spontaneous magical motion.
- A (Awareness): visible gauge/map/coins/status display actively monitored.
- U (Use-when-needed): monitoring tool is closed, holstered or checked only when needed.

Rules:
1. No type may look morally superior, richer, poorer, smarter or more successful.
2. Do not depict literal debt, poverty, bankruptcy, gambling, luxury-brand logos, currency brands or financial institutions.
3. Each character needs a distinct silhouette at ~140px mobile display. Pixel structure must still read at that size; never rely on tiny decorative detail.
4. Transparent background. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI labels or type code inside art.
5. Square master composition, centered full/three-quarter body, generous transparent margin.
6. Keep facial expression readable on mobile.
7. Same universe, pixel scale, rendering, outline language, lighting and proportions across all eight.
8. Do not generate eight palette swaps. Pose + silhouette + prop + motif must differ.

## B. MASTER STYLE PROMPT
Create a premium original Japanese fantasy RPG character for the household-finance game “Mudagiri” in a HIGH-QUALITY MODERN PIXEL-ART style. The pixel language must be immediately visible: deliberate pixel clusters, stepped diagonal edges, limited-but-rich shading ramps, crisp sprite-like highlights, readable pixel texture and controlled dithering where useful. Do NOT render as smooth anime, glossy gacha illustration, painterly 2.5D art, vector art, or merely place a pixel filter over a smooth illustration. Aim for the feeling of a beloved 16-bit/32-bit era JRPG character reimagined with modern high-resolution pixel craftsmanship: expressive face, compact heroic proportions, bold readable silhouette, playful but trustworthy rather than childish, and enough detail for a Result hero while remaining recognizable around 140px on a smartphone. Full or three-quarter body centered in a square composition with generous clear space around the silhouette. Transparent background only. No scenery, frame, card, typography, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, real-world brand or currency symbol. Keep all eight characters in one cohesive sprite-art universe with consistent pixel scale, outline weight, proportions, lighting direction and shading discipline. Do not imply wealth, poverty, intelligence, moral virtue, financial success or failure through clothing quality.

Use this MASTER STYLE for every type, then append exactly one type-specific semantic prompt below.

## C. Type prompts

### FPA — 鉄壁マネー要塞
Semantic target: Future + Plan + Awareness.
Append:
A vigilant fortress-guardian archetype. Stable squared stance, compact shield shaped with orderly layered segments, a small open tactical ledger or glowing status gauge clearly being monitored, neatly organized utility pouches, subtle protective storage motif behind the shoulders like a miniature fortress battlement. Calm confident expression, not smug. Symmetrical and highly structured silhouette. Visual feeling: “I look ahead, make a plan, and usually know what is happening.” Avoid crowns, treasure piles, excessive armor, or anything implying this type is the best or richest.

### FPU — 穴あき家計の番人
Semantic target: Future + Plan + Use-when-needed.
Append:
A forward-looking watch-guardian with a carefully planned defensive kit, but one small intentionally open gap in the layered shield or watchtower motif. Holds a rolled plan/map ready for future use while the monitoring ledger/gauge is closed or clipped at the belt rather than actively watched. Alert expression noticing something slightly late, competent rather than foolish. Structured silhouette with one memorable asymmetry. Visual feeling: “I plan ahead, but I check the small flow when I need to.” The gap is a charming blind spot, not damage, poverty, incompetence or failure.

### FIA — 未来キープストッカー
Semantic target: Future + Intuition + Awareness.
Append:
A nimble future-keeper carrying a protected glowing seed/capsule or small reserve satchel representing something saved for later. One hand actively checks a small visible gauge while the body leans into an instinctive next move. Organized enough to know the flow, but with organic curved gear and a less rigid silhouette than the planner types. Thoughtful but lively expression. Visual feeling: “The future matters, but I still trust my feel when choosing.” Avoid hoarding imagery or piles of money.

### FIU — 未来直感ゾンビ
Semantic target: Future + Intuition + Use-when-needed.
Append:
A lovable fantasy “zombie-like” future wanderer, clearly cute and non-horror: slightly sleepy eyes, tousled silhouette, instinctively walking toward a distant guiding glow while carrying a small future-reserve pack. A closed pocket ledger or dormant gauge hangs unused until needed. Dynamic off-balance-but-purposeful pose, spontaneous gear arrangement, gentle humor. Visual feeling: “I think about the future, choose by feel, and check afterward when necessary.” No gore, decay, injury, frightening undead realism, poverty cues or stupidity caricature.

### VPA — メリハリ消費の賢者
Semantic target: Value-now + Plan + Awareness.
Append:
A lively present-focused sage-adventurer enjoying a small symbolic experience token while simultaneously holding an open, orderly planning board or visible spending gauge. Relaxed composed stance, clean geometric accessories mixed with warm playful details. Expression shows deliberate enjoyment: pleased, aware, in control, never austere. Visual feeling: “I enjoy now, plan the range, and know the flow.” Do not make the character elderly by default; “sage” means balanced judgment, not age or superiority.

### VPU — ご褒美予算ブレイカー
Semantic target: Value-now + Plan + Use-when-needed.
Append:
A cheerful reward-loving adventurer with a neatly prepared small budget pouch or planned token slots, reaching enthusiastically toward one extra glowing reward just outside the planned set. The tracking ledger/gauge is closed and tucked away. Playful “oops, tempting” expression, not shameful. Structured base silhouette disrupted by one energetic forward gesture. Visual feeling: “I set a budget and enjoy it; the fine check comes later.” No shopping-brand imagery, debt, broken piggy bank or financial disaster symbolism.

### VIA — 直感消費エンターテイナー
Semantic target: Value-now + Intuition + Awareness.
Append:
A charismatic motion-filled entertainer-adventurer mid-spin or mid-step, spontaneously choosing between floating experience tokens while an open wrist-like gauge or magical status dial remains clearly visible and understood. Asymmetric flowing silhouette, expressive grin, confident awareness without careful planning props. Visual feeling: “I move by instinct and enjoy the moment, but I usually know what is happening.” No celebrity/luxury cues, casino imagery or reckless-loss symbolism.

### VIU — 感情課金バーサーカー
Semantic target: Value-now + Intuition + Use-when-needed.
Append:
A high-energy fantasy berserker mascot charging joyfully toward a glowing experience/reward motif, with expressive emotion and strong diagonal motion. A small closed ledger/gauge is strapped safely behind or at the belt, signaling it will be checked later. Bold irregular silhouette, energetic hair/cloth motion, excited but friendly face. Visual feeling: “I act on the feeling now and check the flow when I need to.” Make it humorous and relatable, never violent toward people, financially ruined, addicted, stupid or morally bad. No real purchase UI, loot-box UI, casino or gambling imagery.
