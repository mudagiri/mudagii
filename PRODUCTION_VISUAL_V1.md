## Mascot — ムダギリくん
- Yellow chick
- Red headband, knot/tails to viewer-right
- Grey axe with brown handle
- Orange beak and feet
- Thick black pixel outline
- Bright retro JRPG / Famicom-to-early-SFC visual grammar
- Cute/approachable, but capable of fighting; never a generic corporate mascot or human hero

## Brand personality
- Adult-friendly yuru-character × retro RPG × light poison/tough love
- Core line: 「好きなものは、斬らない。ムダだけ、斬る。」
- Never shame spending categories or the user.
- The diagnosis engine is authoritative. Visuals and dialogue must render its outcome, never override it.

## Engine → visual state
- `defeat` → ATTACK / 討伐
- `spared` → NO ATTACK / 守る
- `needs_review` → 要鑑定
- no meaningful issue → CLEAR

## Audience guardrails
- Works for single working men, single working women, couples, and future-family households.
- Do not make the product childlike, gender-coded, or “extreme saving” themed.
- Food, leisure, beauty/fashion, housing, education, and self-development are not evil by category.

## Production sprite contract
- Mascot: idle / talk / attack / clear / review
- Enemy: one textless transparent idle sprite per official category
- Consistent pixel scale, black outline, no baked text, no checkerboard, no background.
- Reference poster is a style/world guide; poster crops do NOT qualify as final transparent sprites.
